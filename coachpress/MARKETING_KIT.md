## 📈 2. Comprehensive Marketing Strategy

### **A. Core Positioning**
Don't sell "Coaching." Sell **"Strategic Outcomes."**
Move your language away from the *process* (sessions, calls) and toward the *infrastructure* (ROI, transformation, efficiency).

### **B. Organic Content Pillars (LinkedIn/Twitter)**
1.  **The "Outcome" Manifesto:** Why your specific framework is the only way to achieve X result.
2.  **Authority Deep-Dives:** Take one Case Study from your site and explain the "hidden" strategy behind it.
3.  **The Comparison:** Show why your premium approach is safer/faster than the "cheap" alternative.

### **C. Lead Magnet Ideas**
-   **The Authority Audit:** A checklist to help clients see where they are losing money.
-   **The Blueprint:** A 1-page PDF showing your 4-step process (based on the Processes section of your site).
