# CoachPress: 30-Day Social Strategy & Content Calendar

This comprehensive strategy is designed to build authority, nurture trust, and drive traffic to your CoachPress site.

---

## 📈 1. The Passive Funnel Strategy

**Goal:** Turn social media attention into a predictable lead flow.

### The Content-to-Conversion Path:
1.  **Top of Funnel (Discovery):** Share valuable "How-To" or "Thought Leadership" posts on LinkedIn/Instagram.
2.  **Middle of Funnel (Nurture):** Guide users to your **Case Study** or **Portfolio** pages.
3.  **Bottom of Funnel (Conversion):** Use "Direct Call" posts that point to your **Premium Sales Letter** or **Contact** page.

---

## 🗓 2. 30-Day Content Calendar

| Week | Monday (Authority) | Wednesday (The Framework) | Friday (Social Proof) | Sunday (Reflection) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## ✍️ 3. Conversational Captions & Non-Salesy Posts

### Sample Conversational Caption:
> "I recently noticed a recurring pattern in the executive consultations I've been running: [Common Pain Point]. Most people think the solution is [Standard Fix], but it's actually [Your Unique Fix]. I've broken down exactly how we implement this in my new Case Study: [Your Link]. What's your take on this approach?"

### The "Non-Salesy" Outreach Strategy:
Instead of pitching, **provide proof.**
- **Post idea:** "Just wrapped a session where we saw [Result]. If you're struggling with [Problem], I've uploaded the framework we used to my site: [Link]."
