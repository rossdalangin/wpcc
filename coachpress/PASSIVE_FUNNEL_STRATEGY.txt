PASSIVE FUNNEL STRATEGY FOR COACHPRESS
--------------------------------------

1. TOP OF FUNNEL (TRAFFIC)
Content strategy: LinkedIn and Pinterest.
- Share 'Authority Hacks' (e.g., '3 ways to fix a low-converting homepage') on LinkedIn.
- Pin 'Beautiful Coaching Website Inspiration' on Pinterest using the CoachPress demo screenshots.
- Use the 30-day authority content calendar to drive traffic to the 'Blog' section of the demo site.

2. MIDDLE OF FUNNEL (LEAD MAGNET)
Concept: The 'Authority Site Audit' or 'Authority Engine Checklist.'
- A free 5-page PDF that guides them through checking their current site for conversion gaps.
- CTA at the end: 'Want a site that already has all this built-in? See the CoachPress demo.'
- Host the lead magnet on a dedicated landing page built with the CoachPress 'About' or 'Services' template.

3. CALL-TO-ACTION STRATEGY
- Use the 'Header CTA' for the main 'Shop CoachPress' link.
- Use the 'Section CTA' (the 'Elite Network' card) in the Team and Service sections to lead directly to the purchase page.
- Add 'In-Article' CTAs in the blog posts using the `[coachpress_section id="cta"]` shortcode.

4. AUTOMATED SALES FLOW
- Step 1: Opt-in for the Checklist.
- Step 2: Confirmation email with the PDF + a 'P.S. Have you seen the new CoachPress 1.5.0 demo yet?' link.
- Step 3: Day 2 Email—Educational. 'The tech bottleneck is costing you clients. Here's why.'
- Step 4: Day 4 Email—Case Study. 'How a boutique consultancy launched their site in 60 seconds.'
- Step 5: Day 7 Email—The Offer. 'Limited time bonus (e.g., free setup guide) if you buy CoachPress this week.'

5. NO-ADS TRAFFIC
- Consistent posting using the 30-day calendar.
- Guest blogging on sites that cater to coaches.
- Active engagement in 'CoachTech' Facebook groups or Reddit communities without hard-selling—just answering technical questions and showing 'what's possible.'
