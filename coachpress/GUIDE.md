# 📖 CoachPress Master Setup Guide

Welcome to CoachPress! This guide is designed for non-technical users to help you build a world-class consulting website with ease.

---

## 🏗 1. The Foundation: Basic Setup

### A. Navigation Menu
1.  Go to **Appearance > Menus**.
2.  Create a new menu named "Main Menu".
3.  Add your pages (Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact).
4.  Check the box that says **"Primary Menu"** in the Menu Settings at the bottom.

### B. Logo & Branding
1.  Go to **Appearance > Customize > Site Identity**.
2.  Upload your logo. We recommend a PNG with a transparent background.
3.  Set your **Site Title** and **Tagline**. These appear in search engine results.

---

## 🧩 2. The Builder: Managing Your Homepage

CoachPress doesn't use heavy, complicated plugins. Instead, we use a **Section Builder** built directly into WordPress.

### How to use the Section Builder:
1.  Go to **Appearance > Customize > Homepage Sections > Manage All Sections**.
2.  **Toggle Visibility:** Use the checkboxes to hide or show sections (like "Testimonials" or "FAQs").
3.  **Drag and Drop:** Click and hold the section boxes to move them up or down.
    - *Example:* If you want your "Partners" right under the "Hero" section, just drag them to the top.

### Customizing Section Content:
Click on any specific section (e.g., **"Hero Section"**) to change:
- **Title & Description:** Layman's terms for what your visitors will read.
- **Backgrounds:** You can choose a solid color, a professional gradient, or upload an image/video.
- **Alignment:** Choose if you want your text on the Left, Right, or Center.

---

## 🗃 3. Content Engines: Services, Team & Portfolio

CoachPress uses "Custom Post Types" to keep your site organized. This means your business information is kept separate from your blog posts.

### How to add items:
- **Services:** Go to **Services > Add New**. Each service gets its own card on the home page and its own detail page.
- **Portfolio:** Go to **Portfolio > Add New**. Use the "Project Category" field to categorize your work.
- **Team Members:** Go to **Team > Add New**. Upload a headshot as the "Featured Image".

**Pro Tip:** If you don't select specific items in the Customizer, the theme will automatically show the newest items you've added.

---

## ⚡️ 4. Theme Power Tools

Found in **Appearance > Customize > Theme Power Tools**, these are for quick actions:

-   **Add Sample Data:** Click this once to populate your site with professional consulting text. It's the fastest way to start.
-   **Reset Database:** This deletes all theme-specific content. Only use this if you want to start over from scratch!

## 🚀 5. The Premium Sales Letter Template

For high-ticket offers, use our specialized **Premium Sales Letter** template.

### How to set it up:
1.  **Create a New Page:** Go to **Pages > Add New**.
2.  **Select Template:** In the **Page Attributes** box on the right, choose **"Premium Sales Letter"** from the Template dropdown.
3.  **Publish:** Click the Publish button.
4.  **Customize:** Go to **Appearance > Customize > Page Templates > Premium Sales Letter**.
5.  **Write Your Copy:** Populate the fields for **Salutation**, **Main Headline**, **Narrative**, and **Pain Points**.
6.  **Add Your CTA:** Set your **CTA Button Text** and **URL** to point to your booking calendar.

## 🎨 6. Style Definitions (In Plain English)

Under **Global Styles**, you can define your visual brand:

-   **Primary Brand Color:** This should be your most dominant color. Think "Blue Chip" Navy or "Eco" Green.
-   **Accent/Highlight Color:** A color that "pops" (like Gold or Orange). Use this sparingly to draw attention to buttons.
-   **Cards:** These are the "boxes" that hold your services or testimonials. You can change their shadow, border roundness (12px is modern, 0px is sharp/classic), and padding.

## 🛠 6. Advanced Customization

### Shortcodes
You can place a homepage section anywhere on your site using this simple code:
`[coachpress_section id="services"]`

### Modal CTA
The button in the header can be set to "Open Modal". This is great for a "Quick Contact" form. You can paste a shortcode from a form plugin (like Contact Form 7) into the **Header Call-to-Action** settings.

## 🎨 Master Your Brand Identity

CoachPress utilizes an intelligent, high-contrast color palette:
*   **Primary Navy (#0D2F4F):** Applied to headers, footers, and large section backgrounds to establish authority.
*   **Accent Gold (#FFC107):** Reserved for primary CTA buttons and critical highlights to draw the eye.
*   **Auto-Contrast Logic:** The theme's Customizer will automatically suggest Navy text on light backgrounds and White text on dark backgrounds to ensure your content is always readable.
