# CoachPress: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## 👤 Admin SOP: Initial Setup & Maintenance

### 1. Installation
1.  **Log in** to your WordPress admin panel.
2.  Navigate to **Appearance > Themes**.
3.  Click **Add New** and then **Upload Theme**.
4.  Upload the `coachpress.zip` file and click **Activate**.

### 2. Site Population (The 60-Second Setup)
1.  Go to **Appearance > Customize**.
2.  Navigate to **Theme Settings > Theme Power Tools**.
3.  Click the **"Add Sample Data"** button. This will populate your site with a professional, high-converting framework.

### 3. Style Selection
1.  In the Customizer, go to **Global Styles > Style Presets**.
2.  Choose a preset (e.g., **Blue Chip**, **Innovator**, **Modernist**, **Luxury**, or **Tech**) to automatically apply professional font and color pairings.

### 4. Updating the Theme
1.  Always **backup your database** before updating.
2.  If you have custom CSS, ensure it's in the **Additional CSS** section or a child theme to avoid loss.

## ✍️ Editor SOP: Managing Content

### 1. Adding New Case Studies & Portfolio Items
1.  Navigate to **Case Studies** or **Portfolio** in the WP sidebar.
2.  Add a **Featured Image** (this is critical for the grid view).
3.  Fill in the **Title** and **Content**.
4.  In the **Sidebar CTA Settings** meta box (if applicable), configure the call-to-action for that specific item.

### 2. Publishing Insights (Blog)
1.  Go to **Posts > Add New**.
2.  Assign a **Category** (e.g., Strategy, Leadership, Operations) for proper organization in the blog grid.
3.  Use **H2 and H3 tags** to maintain the professional typography of the theme.

---

## 📈 Marketer SOP: Conversion Optimization

### 1. Reordering the Homepage
1.  Navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Homepage Sections > Section Ordering**.
2.  Drag and drop the sections (e.g., Hero, Problem, Services, Sales Letter) into the strategic order that fits your current marketing goal.

### 2. Customizing the Sales Letter
1.  Create a new page and select the **Premium Sales Letter** template.
2.  In the Customizer, go to **Page Templates > Premium Sales Letter**.
3.  Populate the **Salutation**, **Headline**, **Narrative**, and **Pain Points** fields with your conversion copy.
4.  Ensure the **CTA Button URL** points to your calendar or booking page.

---

## 📂 Section & Page Explanation

- **Hero Section:** First impression. Clearly states the "What" and "Who" with a primary call to action.
- **Problem Section:** Agitates the user's pain points. Essential for creating a "Need" for your service.
- **About Preview:** Positions the coach as the "Guide" with the plan to solve the client's problem.
- **Services/Processes:** Explains the "How" in a clear, card-based layout.
- **Trust (Partners/Testimonials):** Social proof. Essential for reducing perceived risk in high-ticket transactions.
- **Case Studies:** Proof of results. The final piece of authority-building.
- **Premium Sales Letter:** An immersive, long-form reading experience designed to drive the user to a single conversion point.
